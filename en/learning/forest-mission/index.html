Forest Mission — Explore the forests of Greece

An interactive GIS exploration game that turns the map of Greece into a learning mission: discover forests and ecosystems, complete challenges and learn how to read the natural landscape.

  • Students, families, educators and nature lovers
  • 10–20 minutes

At a glance

  • Real interactive map
  • Solo or two-team play
  • Short GIS missions and questions
  • Collect Dasologio Coins
  • No installation — runs in the browser
Play now

Enter the map and start the mission

Forest Mission is a browser-based educational game combining geography, forest knowledge and landscape observation. Players move across a real map, identify areas of interest and complete short missions that connect place with knowledge.

What you will do

  • Explore Greek forests and forest ecosystems on an interactive map.
  • Answer questions and short observation challenges.
  • Collect Dasologio Coins as you progress.
  • Choose solo play or a two-team experience.

There is nothing to install. The game opens directly in the browser and is designed for learning activities, events, families and anyone who wants a more interactive way to discover Greece’s forests. The current in-game interface is primarily in Greek.
